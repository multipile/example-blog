The pages a reader sees: the list of posts, each post, and how its text and dates are shown.

What belongs here is everything drawn in the browser: pages, components, styles, and how a value is formatted for a reader. The data comes from the blog's API, so no query, schema or password check belongs here.

Read this pile to find out what a reader sees, and how. Change it when a page, or what is shown on one, changes.
