The few small types every part of the blog passes to another, so that each reads them the same way.

What belongs here is a type or a function at least two parts need, with no dependency of its own: a result that may be an error, an instant in time and how one is read. Anything only one part uses stays in that part, and nothing here talks to the network, the database or the page.

Read this pile to find out what shape a value has when it crosses from one part to another. Change it when two parts need to agree on a new shape.
