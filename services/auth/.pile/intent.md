Proves who an author is, to every part of the blog that asks: by password in a browser, and by token from a script.

What belongs here is everything that turns a credential into an author: hashing and checking passwords, starting, finding and ending sessions, issuing and revoking tokens, and the small HTTP service that answers them. What an author may do once known does not belong here, and neither does anything a reader sees.

Read this pile to find out how an author proves who they are, and how long that lasts. Change it when a new way to prove it is added, or when one changes.
