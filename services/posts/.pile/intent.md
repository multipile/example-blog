Serves the blog's posts to anyone who asks: the published ones, newest first, and each one whole.

What belongs here is the HTTP API the pages read posts from, and the queries behind it. Only what a reader may see is served: a draft never leaves this service. Signing in, and what is drawn on a page, do not belong here.

Read this pile to find out what the pages can ask for and what they get back. Change it when a page needs to ask for posts in a new way.
